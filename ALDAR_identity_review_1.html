00  ·  The find

The name already contains the strategy.

Arabic lexicographers derive دار — a house — from the root د‑و‑ر, dāra: to turn, to encircle, to return. A house is called dār because it encircles those inside it.

From the same three letters come دورة, a cycle or a round; دوران, circulation; الدورة الدموية, the circulatory system; and دورة علاجية, a course of treatment. The idea of return — the central promise of rehabilitation — is not something we need to attach to this brand. It is already inside the word.

This also settles the brief's ADR question. A‑D‑R rearranged is D‑A‑R, and D‑A‑R is not a monogram device. It is the root. Each of the three directions that follow answers the ADR question differently, and none of them forces it.

00  ·  Six territories, not six logos

What kind of institution should ALDAR visually become?

Six different answers to that question. They differ in letterform system, in how Arabic and Latin relate, in colour, and in what they ask the viewer to feel first. They are not variants of each other.

01–03 were developed from the brief. 04 is the board you supplied, drawn out into a full system and tested against the brief's own criteria — including where it collides with them. 05 takes the discus turn as its instrument, English-first. 06 puts Arabic in front and leaves it there.

01

THE DATUM  الخط المرجعي

ALDAR is the fixed reference point against which recovery in this region is measured.

AuthoritativeTimelessPreciseRegionalEnduring

The idea

A datum is the surveyed line every other measurement is taken from — in architecture, in engineering, and in medicine, where a baseline reading is the thing every later result is compared against. This direction does not claim that ALDAR is caring, or advanced, or world-class. It claims something harder to argue with: that ALDAR is the reference.

How it is built

The wordmark is cut as one monolithic block. Every letter has a flat top, so the silhouette is a strict rectangle broken only by the R's leg. A single horizontal — the datum, at 78% of cap height — runs through the crossbars of both A's, the springing of the L's arm and the floor of the D's counter. The R is the only letter that crosses it. Verticals 24, horizontals 22, on a cap of 100. There are no curves except the D and R bowls, and both are true semicircles.

The ADR question

Declined as a device. Used as a construction rule.

A, D and R are the three structural forms in the name; the L is the datum stood on end and the second A is its rhythm. A separate ADR monogram would give the institution a second thing to ask people to remember. The wordmark is the equity. The ADR lockup is shown here as the study it is, not as a proposal.

Strategic advantages

  • Authority is the hardest thing to buy and this route has the most of it.
  • Cheap to execute well — it needs restraint, not budget.
  • Ages at the rate of stone. Credible in 2040 without revision.
  • Reads as infrastructure to ministries, insurers and referring hospitals.

Strategic risks

  • Emotionally cool. Longevity and performance clients may find it forbidding.
  • At a glance, a heavy geometric wordmark can be mistaken for property or construction — the palette and Arabic partner do most of the work of preventing this.
  • Requires a client who will police it. Loosely applied, it just looks bold and generic.

Why a competitor cannot easily own this

A competitor can commission a heavy grotesque. They cannot easily commission the flat-topped A and the 0.78 datum without producing a visible copy, because those two decisions are what make the silhouette recognisable at 30 metres. The defensible asset here is a proportion, not a picture.

Partner discussion
What would choosing this direction say about ALDAR?
That ALDAR intends to be judged as an institution rather than liked as a clinic, and is confident enough not to illustrate what it does.
What type of patient would it attract most naturally?
Complex and post-operative cases, and the families choosing on competence rather than comfort. It is the route that most reassures someone who is frightened.
What does it communicate to physicians?
Protocol, measurement, and an institution that will not embarrass them in a referral letter.
What does it communicate to athletes?
That this is where you are repaired, not where you are celebrated. It reads as the medical team behind the team.
What does it communicate to government and institutional partners?
A credible counterparty. It looks like something that can hold a licence, publish outcomes and sign a national contract.
What might be misunderstood?
Coldness read as indifference; and on first glance, a possible confusion with real-estate or engineering brands.
What is the greatest strategic advantage?
It buys institutional permission early — ALDAR can act like a reference institution before it has the scale of one.
What is the greatest strategic risk?
It under-serves the consumer end of the business, where longevity and performance clients are choosing partly on desire.
How easily could a competitor imitate it?
The register is imitable; the specific letterforms are not. Medium risk, and visible when it happens.
What would this identity enable ALDAR to become in ten years?
A hospital group, a training institute and a publishing body — the things that need gravity more than warmth.
02

RANGE  المدى

ALDAR is where human capability is measured, extended and given back.

HumanScientificIntelligentDynamicOptimistic

The idea

Rehabilitation has a currency and it is not comfort — it is range. Degrees of motion, metres walked, kilos loaded, weeks to return. The goniometer is the instrument of this profession and almost nobody has built a brand from it. This direction treats measurement as the emotional story: progress you can see is the thing patients actually hold on to.

How it is built

One stroke weight, one family of radii, open apertures. The A's apex is a true radius rather than a point, the D's bowl is a half-circle, and the R's leg is an arc rather than a straight. The sweep increases across the word — the A carries a shallow arc, the D a half turn, the R the fullest travel — so ALDAR opens as it is read. It is condensed where Direction 01 is wide, which is what keeps the two apart at a glance.

THE ROOT  الجذر

ALDAR is an Arab medical institution that does not translate itself.

ArabContemporaryCulturedMedicalDistinctive

The idea

Arabic lexicographers derive دار — a house — from the root د‑و‑ر, dāra: to turn, to encircle, to return. A house is called dār because it encircles those inside it. From the same root come دورة, a cycle or a round; دوران, circulation; الدورة الدموية, the circulatory system — and دورة علاجية, a course of treatment. The idea of return is already inside the name. This is also the honest answer to the ADR question: A-D-R is D-A-R, and D-A-R is not a monogram, it is the root.

How it is built

One pen, two scripts. Both alphabets are cut with a broad nib held at 32°, the Arabic writing angle, so the Latin inherits Arabic stroke logic instead of the reverse: modulated weight, angled entries, terminals cut on the pen rather than square to the baseline. And the name ends on the same letter in both scripts — R and ر — which in both is the only letter that leaves the line. The name ends in motion in either language.

THE SIGNATURE  التوقيع

ALDAR is the address people choose when they could go anywhere, and it never raises its voice.

DiscreetComposedPremiumInternationalQuiet

The idea

This is the direction already on your board, drawn out into a full system. Its logic is that two of the five letters are the same shape: the A is a triangle standing up and the D is the same triangle lying on its side. Everything else — the hairline weight, the wide tracking, the champagne on forest green — exists to make the brand behave like a piece of jewellery rather than a piece of signage.

How it is built

Monoline at 3.6% of cap height, tracked at 0.34 em. A and D are pure triangles; the L is reduced to two strokes; the R keeps a circular bowl so the word does not become a puzzle. The A carries no crossbar in the primary lockup, exactly as on the supplied board — a low-crossbar cut is drawn here as well and should be mandatory below 20 mm and in all signage.

The ADR question

Neither embedded nor declined. Converted into a shape.

Rather than binding A, D and R as letters, this route makes A and D the same geometric object and lets the R stay a letter. The monogram that results is a pair of triangles rather than a set of initials — which is why it can be used as a pattern, a frame and a perforation without ever spelling anything.

Symbol

The two triangles, locked.

The D triangle held inside the A triangle. It is the one element of this route that survives reduction: the wordmark itself does not, because a hairline at 16 px is a grey smudge. Any use below 25 mm should be the triangle mark with the name set beside it, not the wordmark reduced.

  • Strongest of the six for self-pay longevity clients and international patients.
  • Sits comfortably beside luxury hospitality and private banking, which is the company ALDAR's highest-value patients keep.
  • The two-triangle construction is a genuine idea and is worth keeping in any outcome.

Strategic risks

  • It collides with the brief's own exclusions. Hairline gold on forest is the visual language of hotels, spas and cosmetic clinics, and section 6 asks ALDAR not to resemble any of those.
  • It fails the airport test and the minimum-size test. A hairline mark disappears at 30 metres and fills in at 12 mm; the identity would need a separate signage weight to function at all.
  • Gold is doing the work. Section 15 sets the standard — the mark must be complete in pure black and white — and in black and white this route loses most of its distinction.
  • A crossbar-less A weakens the readability the brief calls non-negotiable.
  • Medical credibility is the weakest of the six. Nothing here says that serious clinical work happens inside.

Why a competitor cannot easily own this

Least defensible of the six. A hairline geometric with a triangular D is a style, and styles are available to any competitor who hires a good type designer — several Gulf wellness brands are already in this territory. The ownable part is the two-triangle construction; the rest is rented. If the partners love this route, the honest path is to keep the triangles, add a signage weight, restore the crossbar and move gold to an accent — at which point it becomes a considerably stronger and more original brand.

THE TURN  الدوران

ALDAR is where the body is taught to turn again.

KineticScientificEnglish-firstAthleticRooted

The idea

The discus is the oldest image in sport of a human body making force out of rotation — and rotation is also the literal root of this brand's name: دار, يدور, to turn. This direction puts those two facts into one letter. The D of ALDAR becomes the instrument: its bowl is the turn, and its lower line is the thrower's arm, leaving the circle on the tangent at the moment of release. Recovery, in this reading, is not rest. It is the restoration of the body's ability to generate force.

How it is built

A geometric grotesque at a single stroke weight. The D's bowl is a true semicircle, which means the tangent at its lowest point is exactly horizontal — so the arm is not drawn at a chosen angle, it is simply where the circle stops turning and the line begins. In the wordmark the arm overshoots the bowl by one fifth of the cap height. In the system it runs to any length you need. English leads by construction: the idea lives in a Latin letter.

ALDAR is an Arab institution whose name is Arabic — in Riyadh, and in London.

ArabMonumentalCivicConfidentLegible

The idea

In this direction the logo is الدار. Not the Arabic version of the logo — the logo. English appears underneath it, smaller, in a quiet grotesque, as what it actually is: a translation for people who need one. Every other route in this document asks how Arabic should accompany Latin. This one declines the question. It is the most confident position available to a brand whose name means something in Arabic and nothing in English.

How it is built

A modular geometric Kufi, drawn from a single square module. Every stroke is one module wide, every junction is a right angle, and there is not one curve in the mark. Three uprights — alif, lam, alif — set the rhythm; the dal's bar runs along the baseline and turns up at its end; the ra drops below the line and turns left. The word is built the way a wall is built, which is what Kufi was for.

What the scores actually say

06 First Language, 03 The Root and 01 The Datum finish inside a quarter of a point of each other, which in an exercise this subjective is not a gap. What separates them is not quality but posture: 01 asks to be read as an institution, 03 as an institution that is Arab, 06 as Arab first and institutional second.

05 The Turn finishes just behind them with the sharpest single idea and the best symbol, held back only by its deliberately English-led treatment of Arabic. 02 Range is the most conventional answer — most likely to be liked, least likely to be remembered.

04 The Signature scores lowest, and it deserves a fair reading. It is the most immediately premium of the six and the partners already know it. It scores low here because this scorecard is the brief's, and the brief weights institutional authority, distinctiveness and scalability at 40% combined — the three things a hairline gold wordmark is worst at. Weight the card for desirability and self-pay appeal instead and 04 moves up sharply. The question is not whether it is good. It is whether ALDAR is being built to be admired or to be trusted.

The trade-offs worth arguing about

  • Authority against ownership. 01 borrows authority from a language institutions already speak. 06 builds authority nobody else can borrow back.
  • Now against later. 02 and 05 perform best in the next three years, on phones and in apps. 01, 03 and 06 perform best over fifteen, on buildings and in memory.
  • Admiration against trust. 04 is the most desirable and the least credible; 01 is the most credible and the least desirable. Neither is wrong — they answer different business plans.
  • Which language ALDAR thinks in. 05 thinks in English and translates. 06 thinks in Arabic and translates. 03 refuses to choose. This is the real decision.
  • Craft budget. 06 and 03 are the most expensive to finish properly and the least forgiving of a rushed rollout. That is a constraint, not a design opinion.

If you want a hybrid

The two that combine cleanly are 06 + 05: the Arabic-first hierarchy of 06 with the Turn Mark of 05 as the symbol, since one is a wordmark strategy and the other is a symbol strategy. 01 + 04 also works — the Datum's proportions at the Signature's palette. Combining more than two dilutes all of them.

What happens after this review

Decide the territory. Then we go deep on one.

1  Territory decision

Partners choose one direction, or ask for one hybrid — normally the strategy of one with the letterform system of another. Anything beyond a single hybrid dilutes all three.

2  Refinement

Six to eight weeks: bespoke Arabic cut with an Arabic lettering specialist, final Latin curves redrawn as true Bézier outlines, full type licensing, colour proofed on stone, paper, powder-coat and screen.

3  System build

Environmental, clinical documentation, digital design system in both scripts, apparel, iconography, photography commission, and the brand book that keeps it intact after we leave.
